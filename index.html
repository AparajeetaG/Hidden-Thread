About Hidden Thread

Hidden Thread is an independently created daily word game built around original themes and puzzles. Its first season contains 60 daily boards: 20 Easy, 20 Moderate, and 20 Hard.

The goal is simple: make a thoughtful, satisfying word puzzle available to everyone for free.

Privacy

Effective July 24, 2026. This version does not require an account and does not send your puzzle progress to a server. Statistics, settings, and completion history are stored only in your browser through local storage.

No advertising or analytics code is active in this release. This notice must be updated before third-party advertising, analytics, contact forms, or other data-collecting services are enabled.
